rate_my_library.

Turns a listening history into a queue you can rate quickly, then hands you an ordered list ready for RateYourMusic. It reads a Last.fm scrobble export or a Spotify extended streaming history, merges the edition variants so each record appears once, and ranks by how much you actually played it.
